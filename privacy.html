BuildMitr – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · BuildMitr

BuildMitr is a construction project management app used by builders and the people they work with — site engineers, architects, clients and office staff. This page explains what information the app keeps and why.

Information we collect

  • Your email address — to send you a 6-digit login code and to know which company and projects you belong to.
  • Name, phone number and role — entered by your company owner on the Team page so colleagues can contact you.
  • Project information you enter — tasks, daily site reports, materials, labour attendance, payments, documents, chat messages and similar work records.
  • Photos and files you upload — such as site photos and drawings.
  • Location — only when you choose to pin a site location on a map. The app does not track your location in the background.

How it is used

  • To run the app: show your projects, share updates with your team and send login codes.
  • We do not sell your information, show advertising or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Records are stored in the company's own Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive) and sent over encrypted (https) connections. Photos and files get long random links; only people who can see the entry in the app are shown the link.

Who can see it

Only members of your company. What each person sees depends on the role and access the company owner gives them (for example, clients see only their own project).

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept while your company uses BuildMitr. You can ask for your account or any of your data to be deleted by writing to the email below; the company owner can also remove you from the Team page, which ends your access straight away.

Children

BuildMitr is a work app and is not meant for children under 13.
